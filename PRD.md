# PRD: JP Consulting Group Website

## 1. Project Identity
- **Company Name:** JP Consulting Group Inc.
- **Industry:** Project Management, with focus on Engineering and Construction Projects. Our services expand in the Mining, Public transit and heavy civil industry. 
- **Tagline:** Strategic Engineering. Technical Integrity.
- **Contact:** julienp@jpconsultinggroup.ca

## 2. Target Audience
- Junior and Senior mining companies seeking technical consulting.
- Legal firms and law firms requiring Expert Witness testimony for construction or engineering lawsuits.
- Investors looking for technical due diligence on mineral projects.

## 3. Visual & Thematic Requirements
- **Color Palette:** Industrial (Dark Grey, Slate, Charcoal) with Gold/Amber accents.
- **Imagery:** High-resolution industrial engineering, gold mining operations, and clean technical blueprints.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, expert, reliable, and strictly professional.

## 4. Website Content (Single Page Structure)
- **Hero Section:** High-impact engineering imagery with the tagline.
- **Expert Witness Services:** Highlight technical testimony, legal report drafting, and lawsuit support.
- **Engineering Consulting:** Construction execution planning, schedule risk mitigation, and project management.
- **Track Record:** Focus on large-scale mining projects (Eskay Creek, Wasamac Feasibility).
- **Contact:** Call to action leading to the professional email.

## 5. Agent Instructions (The "Code" for the AI)
- Use modern, responsive HTML5 and CSS3.
- Ensure the site is mobile-friendly.
- Do not include personal addresses, phone numbers, or insurance specifics.
- Focus on "fully insured professional services."
